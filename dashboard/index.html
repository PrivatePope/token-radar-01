    How the radar values a token that does not exist yet

    Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × age × chains.

    The category multiple is the traded market's own FDV/TVL for that category: the TVL-weighted ratio of sums over its traded peers, shrunk toward the global multiple (0.082) so a thin category cannot set its own price. A category with fewer than five traded peers falls back to the global multiple. Two hygiene rules keep the multiples honest: versioned deployments are dropped from the peer pool (DefiLlama attributes a token's whole market cap to each versioned deployment — Aave V2 alone, $91M of TVL carrying the whole $1.72B AAVE market cap, pushed the Lending multiple to 2.56 before it was dropped), and any peer with FDV/TVL above 5 is dropped. The multiples this snapshot gives: Dexs 1.00, Lending 0.33, Yield 0.25, Basis Trading 0.16, RWA 0.13, Liquid Staking 0.03, everything thin at the global 0.082.

    Growth is 1 + 0.5 × the seven-day TVL change (clamped to −30%…+100%); no change reported means 1. Age is 0.85 + 0.45 × days listed (capped at 4 years) ÷ 1460 — a protocol that has run longer with no token has waited longer, and is worth more. Chains is 1 + 0.03 × ln(chains). The value is capped at 25× TVL. Every input is a snapshot figure; no protocol name is special-cased, and each row carries its full breakdown so any value can be checked from the snapshot alone.
