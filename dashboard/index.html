How the radar values a token

EV = TVL0.8 × 8 × (1 + 0.9 × clamp(7d%, −50..+150)) × (0.6 + 0.7 × min(age, 1460) ÷ 1460) × (1 + 0.015 × min(chains, 10)) × odds(age)

Four signals from the snapshot, and nothing else. Size: total value locked, raised to 0.8 — size has diminishing returns. Momentum: the last seven days' TVL change, clamped. Patience: how many days the protocol has run with no token, up to four years. Reach: how many chains it runs on, up to ten — a launch that spans chains reaches more farmers. The board ranks by expected value: the launch's implied value times the share of protocols of this age that record a token. No listing date takes the median pre-token age (513 days); a missing seven-day change counts as zero. No name is special-cased.

How we know the model works

  • The traded market, fitted. log(market cap) = 6.965 + 0.522 × log(TVL) on 128 traded peers, R² = 0.194. Held out (5-fold): median error ×3.67; only 30% of predictions land within 2×. The traded market's multiples span orders of magnitude, so no formula prices a launch precisely — the board ranks, it does not price.
  • The agreement check. Replacing the hand-set TVL term (TVL0.8 × 8) with the fitted curve leaves the top 8 in the same order, top-10 9/10, Spearman 0.991. Two independent routes to the same ranking.
  • The launch odds. A logistic fit on log age: −3.595 + 0.604 × ln(age). Observed vs predicted by age bucket: 26% vs 27% (0–180 days), 40% vs 45% (180–365), 54% vs 55% (365–730), 64% vs 64% (730–1460), 80% vs 71% (1460+). Brier score 0.229 against a base-rate 0.248.
  • The caveat. The odds are the share of protocols of that age that record a token, not a forward probability that a protocol still waiting will launch. Age is counted twice — once in the patience factor, once in the odds — so the combined age spread is about 7×. The top 8 are unchanged, so the cost is bounded at the top of the board.
